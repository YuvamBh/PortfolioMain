What duvi is

duvi is personal assistant software that runs on a computer owned by its single user, Yuvam Bhargav ("the owner"). It is not a hosted service. There are no accounts, no sign-up and no other users. This policy explains what data the software handles when the owner connects it to Google services.

Google user data duvi accesses

With the owner's consent through Google OAuth, duvi reads and writes the owner's own Gmail messages and drafts, Google Calendar events, and Google Contacts. The access is used only to show the owner their mail and calendar, to draft and, after the owner's explicit approval, send replies and create events, and to recognise the owner's contacts.

Where data is stored

All data stays on the owner's computer, in an encrypted disk and a local database that only the owner can open. Authentication tokens are stored in the computer's system keychain. Nothing is uploaded to any server operated by duvi, because duvi operates no servers.

Sharing

duvi does not sell, share, transfer or disclose Google user data to anyone. Portions of message text may be sent to an AI model provider (Anthropic or OpenAI), under the owner's own accounts with those providers, solely to generate drafts and summaries for the owner. No data is used to train models, for advertising, or for any purpose other than serving the owner.

Limited Use disclosure

duvi's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention and deletion

Data is kept on the owner's computer for as long as the owner finds it useful. The owner can delete any stored message, note or fact at any time from the software's console, revoke Google access at myaccount.google.com/permissions, or delete the local database entirely.
