duvi

A personal assistant that runs on its owner's own computer. It reads the owner's mail, messages and calendars, drafts replies for the owner to approve, and keeps notes the owner can read and edit.

duvi is private software built and operated by Yuvam Bhargav for personal use. It is not offered to the public and has no sign-up.
